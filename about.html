perpahmian.tech is an independent product engineering studio led by Fahmi Bastari in Indonesia.

Why this practice exists

Useful software begins with understanding the work around it—not with choosing a framework.

Many product failures start at the seams: the field workflow nobody mapped, the admin screen treated as an afterthought, the architecture that assumes perfect connectivity, or the hand-off where context disappears.

Our practice keeps those seams visible. Product thinking, interface decisions, and engineering stay connected from the first conversation to the working release.

Founder-led practice

Fahmi Bastari

Founder & Product Engineer

Fahmi leads product definition, interface decisions, and engineering delivery. Clients work directly with the person responsible for shaping and building the product, with specialist collaborators involved when the scope requires them.

This structure keeps context close to implementation and accountability clear from the first conversation through release.

Make complexity legible.
