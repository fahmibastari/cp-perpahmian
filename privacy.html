Privacy, in plain language.

The contact form only asks for the information needed to understand and reply to a project enquiry.

Last updated 29 August 2026.

What is collected

Your name, email address, optional organisation name, and the project context you choose to provide.

How it is used

The information is used only to review your enquiry, reply to you, and continue a potential working conversation. The website does not create a marketing profile from the form.

How it is delivered

The submission is sent through Resend to the perpahmian.tech email inbox. The website does not maintain its own database of form submissions, although email providers may retain messages according to their service policies.

Your choice

Do not include confidential credentials or highly sensitive information in the initial brief. You can request deletion of a conversation by emailing perpahmian.tech@gmail.com.
